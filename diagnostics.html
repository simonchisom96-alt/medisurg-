What this can and cannot verify

A successful profile read confirms that sign-in and this database read permission work for the current browser. It does not prove that every multiplayer write rule, room workflow, leaderboard query, or Pollinations image-generation secret is configured correctly.

Image generation also requires the Cloudflare Pages secret POLLINATIONS_API_KEY and R2 binding MEDISURG_IMAGES.
